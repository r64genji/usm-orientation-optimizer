## 1. Spatial Telemetry Diagnostics: Moving Queues vs. Stationary Standstills

Analyzing GPS data by **spatial displacement over time** (rather than instantaneous speed) reveals that the 63.3-minute commute consists almost entirely of **two distinct queueing traps**:

```text
+------------------------------------------------------+----------+---------------+----------------+----------------+
| Commute Segment                                      | Duration | Net Progress  | Effective Spd  | Queue Drag Loss|
+------------------------------------------------------+----------+---------------+----------------+----------------+
| 1. Restu Moving Queue (Road Shoulder Shuffle)        | 24.1 min | 373 meters    |  0.93 km/h     | 15.0 min (62%) |
| 2. Shuttle Bus Transit (Vehicular Link)              |  3.1 min | 860 meters    | 16.82 km/h     |  0.0 min ( 0%) |
| 3. DTSP Exterior Reservoir (Stationary Road Stand)   | 31.4 min |  99 meters    |  0.19 km/h     | 25.6 min (81%) |
| 4. DTSP Gate Inflow (Hall Ingress Crawl)             |  4.7 min | 121 meters    |  1.55 km/h     |  2.1 min (44%) |
+------------------------------------------------------+----------+---------------+----------------+----------------+
| TOTAL ACTIVE COMMUTE                                 | 63.3 min | 1,453 meters  |  1.38 km/h     | 42.7 min (67%) |
+------------------------------------------------------+----------+---------------+----------------+----------------+
```

### Why Students Were "Walking Slow" (The Peristaltic Moving Queue)
* **Phase 1 was not leisurely walking**: Covering only 373 meters in 24.1 minutes means students advanced at **15.5 meters per minute**.
* **Mechanism**: The queue at the Restu bus stop backed up **400 meters up the road shoulder**. Students were trapped in a single/double-file moving queue. Every time a bus loaded 44 students, the line surged forward 30 meters, then froze for 5–7 minutes until the next bus arrived.
* **Phase 3 was a total standstill**: Outside DTSP, students were held static on the asphalt road under the sun for 31.4 minutes, advancing only 99 meters (**3.1 meters per minute**).
* **Net Inefficiency**: Out of a 63.3-minute commute, **42.7 minutes were lost directly to queueing drag** (67.5% pure loss), all for a **3.1-minute bus ride**.

---

## 2. Root Operational Flaws

1. **Physical Interference (Bus Queue Blockades the Walking Path)**:
   * The 400-meter line of students waiting for the bus occupied the entire paved road shoulder.
   * Students wanting to walk to DTSP were physically blocked by the bus queue or forced into oncoming vehicular traffic on the narrow road.
2. **Artificial Chokepoint at DTSP (NO Security Checks)**:
   * **DTSP has zero security checks** (no bag searches, no metal detectors).
   * Yet marshals only opened 1–2 perimeter doors, admitting ~30–35 students/minute while buses arrived depositing 40–80 students every 5 minutes. The exterior road became an unshaded 400-person holding pen.
3. **Reactive Next-Station Throttling**:
   * When the DTSP road filled, DTSP marshals radioed Restu to pause bus loading, instantly freezing the 400-meter line at Restu.

---

## 3. Four Concrete Management Interventions for Organizers

### Intervention 1: Establish a Dedicated "Walking Express Lane" (Laluan Pantas)
* **Problem**: Students who want to walk are stuck behind the bus line.
* **Solution**: Place traffic cones along the Restu road shoulder to delineate two distinct channels:
  1. **Channel A (Static Queue)**: For students waiting to board shuttle buses.
  2. **Channel B (Express Walkway)**: A clear, unimpeded 1.2-meter pedestrian lane for walking students.
* **Impact**: Enables able-bodied students to walk at full 4.8 km/h (~19–21 min door-to-door), immediately diverting 40–50% of the crowd away from the bus stop.

### Intervention 2: Staged Holding Pens at Restu (Eliminate the 400m Road Queue)
* **Problem**: Standing on the road shoulder for 24 minutes in the heat causes fatigue before orientation even starts.
* **Solution**: Replace the 400-meter roadside line with **batch-staged waiting in shaded areas**:
  * Keep students seated inside the Restu cafeteria / multipurpose hall.
  * Assign color-coded or numbered batch cards (Batch 1, Batch 2, Batch 3 = 44 students each).
  * Only call a batch to the boarding curb when their bus is physically arriving.
* **Impact**: Eliminates roadside queue standing; students wait comfortably seated under fans.

### Intervention 3: Multi-Portal Parallel Ingress at DTSP (Open All 6 Doors)
* **Problem**: 31-minute standstill on the road outside DTSP.
* **Solution**: Since there are **no security checks**, open all 6 exterior perimeter double-doors:
  * **North Doors (Facing Library PHS)**: Dedicated for **Desasiswa Restu & Tekun**.
  * **South Doors (Facing Canselori / Lake)**: Dedicated for **Desasiswa Saujana & Indah Kembara**.
  * **Center / East Doors**: Dedicated for **Aman Damai, Bakti Permai, Cahaya Gemilang**.
* **Throughput Multiplier**: Triples hall ingestion from 30/min to **100–120 students/minute**. Bus drop-offs are absorbed immediately without any exterior accumulation.

### Intervention 4: Staggered Desasiswa Departure Waves
Replace the blanket "everyone be at DTSP by 08:30" with 20-minute staggered arrival waves:
* **Wave 1 (07:45 – 08:00)**: Flat-terrain hostels (Aman Damai, Bakti Permai, Fajar Harapan).
* **Wave 2 (08:00 – 08:15)**: Mid-distance hostels (Indah Kembara, Saujana).
* **Wave 3 (08:15 – 08:30)**: Hilltop hostels (Restu, Tekun).
