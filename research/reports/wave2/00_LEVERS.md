---

## Verdict

Stop releasing coaches because the hostel is “ready.” Release **one bus-sized group** when DTSP says the kerb/foyer can take them. Keep everyone else **sitting in DUD / cafe shade**. Count at the hostel, clicker on the move, MyCSD QR **after they sit**. Open **more hall doors** and do **not** sort hostels on the asphalt.

That is the whole MVP control loop. Walking Restu to the hall is still forbidden.

## Lever pack (what researchers actually found)

### 1. Pull, don’t panic-hold — `11_release_metering.md`

Named analogs (new to this wave): Dover TAP lorry metering, TfL station control, US Army FM 55-65 chalk / call-forward, Hajj Mina→Jamarat quota gates, FEMA STAM “call forward only when destination reports capacity.”

**Ready buffer:** keep **one** coach-load in the air or at the kerb so the hall does not starve. Everyone else stays seated upstream.

**Radio (hypothesis, not a USM SOP):** DTSP calls `READY Batch N, 44 seats` → Restu boards and sends → if foyer surges, `HOLD DISPATCH` while rolling buses still finish.

Today’s USM pattern (field): hold **after** Jalan Perpustakaan is already full. That is the inverse of every analog above.

### 2. Batch = one coach, 2 escorts — `12_batch_size_escorts.md`

| Size | Escorts | USM fit |
|---|---|---|
| 10–25 | 1–2 | No — burns PPSL, underfills coaches |
| **40–44** | **2 (lead + sweeper)** | **Yes** — 1:20–1:22, fills the vehicle |
| 80–90 | 3–4 | Maybe if two bays exist |
| 300–500 whole hostel | 10–20 | No — this *is* the road pile |

**Stragglers:** military “chalk / bump” — late 10% take the **next** bus. Colour token = hostel identity; convoy membership ≠ “wait for Block A’s last person.”

Queueing math in that report: waiting to assemble 500 at ~10 pax/min ≈ **25 min** dead wait before boarding starts; 44-pax transfer batch ≈ **2 min**. Treat the 91% cut as **formula**, not a USM field result.

### 3. Hold in DUD, not on the road — `13_upstream_holding.md`

- DOSH 2016 / Singapore WSH: unshaded static work at WBGT ≥32°C wants **shade rest**, not 45 min on asphalt.
- OSM way `275415002` (M08 DUD): footprint **1,669 m²**. Exam seating documented to seat **278**. Banquet/theatre **500–800** is an **estimate**, not a measured orientation count. Cafe overflow ~250–400 seats typical — verify tomorrow.
- Call-forward: seated reservoir → **exactly 44** in a kerb pen → board when doors open. Last 30 m should be the only sun.

**Do not encode 800–1200 DUD pax as fact.** Pace the floor.

### 4. Don’t QR the kerb — `14_headcount_no_dwell.md`

| Method | s/pax (their sources) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Named roll in foyer shade | 0 extra on the trip | Origin |
| Clicker / colour glance | 0.2–0.5 | Bus door, hall door |
| Phone QR at gate | 3–10 | **Forbidden on the path** |
| MyCSD QR on the **hall screen** after seated | 0 dwell | Attendance credit |

AUKU / 80% rules do **not** require a turnstile. Foyer roster + in-hall MyCSD is enough **if** BHEPA accepts it — still confirm locally.

Clicker error ~5% (TETRA); single-file bus door better.

### 5. Short loop: don’t Daganzo-hold empty buses — `15_bus_ops_levers.md`

- Operator: Unic Leisure. Vehicles: **single-door high-floor tour coaches**, tender language **40 seats**. Route E (RST) **2 units**, 10–15 min in **normal** semester (MPP posts). **MSL extra charters = NOT FOUND.**
- Rapid T310 cannot eat 2000 freshies at 07:30 (3 buses, 20–30 min).
- TCQSM: high-floor single door **~3.0–4.5 s board / 2.0–2.8 s alight**. Marshal-metered column helps; scramble makes it worse.
- Cycle they sketch: board 150 s + run 180 s + alight 100 s + deadhead 180 s ≈ **10 min**. Then 2 coaches without charters **cannot** clear RST in a morning — so either charters exist (unproven) or the current pile is mathematically inevitable.
- **Hold at origin (shade). Do not hold empty buses at DTSP.** Immediate deadhead.

**Contradiction to freeze later:** report 13 says pre-batched board 1.5–2.0 s/pax; report 15 says 3.0–4.5. Use **video 2.5 s/pax (n=1)** as prior until stopwatch.

### 6. Hall must swallow the pulse — `16_destination_absorption.md`

- One 44-pax alight in ~45–60 s. One **fully open** double door (~1.4 m) at Green Guide rates can take that pulse in **~25 s**. One half-leaf + “hostel mana?” at the door drops to **~20–25 pax/min** → road stack.
- **Open ≥2 double doors.** No credential check in the last **20 m** (SGSA Zone Ex analog).
- Sort hostels **inside** with colour banners, not at the threshold.
- UBBL 1984 by-law 182 they quoted: 60 pax/min through doors on the level (legal travel-rate figure — still measure DTSP).
- Foyer as sitting/standing buffer is the legal/comfort place to wait, **if** 1.2 m exit gangways stay clear (by-law 185). Outdoor asphalt is the failure.

Measure tomorrow: clear width of Pintu A/B/C, how many leaves actually open, 60 s door counts.

### 7. Signal + timestamps — `17_signals_failure_modes.md`

- Human radio lag **45–90 s** (not radio physics). WhatsApp **60–180 s** — log only, not the hold line.
- Foyer occupancy ≈ clicker_in − clicker_into_hall. Amber ~100, hard HOLD ~150 (policy priors, not DTSP cert).
- Starvation fix: call `READY` when the **current** group starts entering hall doors, not when foyer is empty (lead time ≈ bus run ~3–5 min).
- **One command:** only DTSP grants dispatch. Restu never self-times. Other hostels same net.
- Five posts, **cumulative** clicker, write the number every 60 s. That is the DES trace: arrived / start service / finish / depart / next-arrive / batch_id.
