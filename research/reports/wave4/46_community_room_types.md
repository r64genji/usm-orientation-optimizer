# Wave 4 — Student & Community Evidence: Real Rooms and Occupancy at Kampus Induk Desasiswa (Focus: Restu Twin Rooms)

**TL;DR:** Independent student first-person testimonies, room tour transcripts, and architectural blogs corroborate that Desasiswa Restu (along with sister complexes Saujana and Tekun in the RST complex) operates standard undergraduate rooms configured for **2 occupants per room**, arranged into 4-room "cubicles" sharing 2 bathrooms and 2 toilets per 8 residents.

---

### Key Findings
- **Desasiswa Restu is 2 pax/room:** First-person student accounts on Threads and independent student blogs explicitly verify that 1 room houses 2 students, organized in a cubicle unit of 4 rooms (8 people) sharing 2 showers and 2 toilets ([Threads @nnnnnnnnnnnayyifah.112]; [monanene.blogspot.com]).
- **RST Architectural Uniformity:** Video room tour transcripts confirm that Desasiswa Restu, Saujana, and Tekun share the identical modular layout where keys are tagged A and B per room ([YouTube / Lina Fadhlina]).
- **Vertical Floor Plan (Restu M02):** Restu M02 has a 10-level split-floor design (5 main physical floor plates split into two tiered half-layers each) serviced by elevators ([monanene.blogspot.com]).
- **Older/Alternative Main Campus Hostels Vary:** While RST is twin-share cubicle style, older traditional hostels like Aman Damai, Bakti Permai, and Fajar Harapan feature direct corridor-access rooms with communal floor bathrooms, with select upgraded rooms sold as Executive/Premium single occupancy ([USM Corporate Accommodation / booking.workplace.com.my]; [myusminfo.com]).
