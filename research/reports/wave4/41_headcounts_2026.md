# Wave 4 Research Report: Official 2026/2027 Headcounts for USM Kampus Induk Desasiswa (MSL 2026)

**TL;DR:** USM Kampus Induk recorded exactly 3,543 first-year students checked into 7 desasiswa from 12–14 September 2026 (Restu: 1,044; Saujana: 610; Tekun: 508; Aman Damai: 410; Cahaya Gemilang Harapan: 386; Bakti Fajar Permai: 341; Indah Kembara: 244), out of 5,804 undergraduate offers university-wide.

---

### Key Findings
- **All 7 Kampus Induk hostels have verified 2026 check-in headcounts** from official BHEPA daily bulletins, totaling 3,543 registered new undergraduates (`official`).
- **Desasiswa Restu was not omitted:** it held registration on Day 3 (14 September 2026) with **1,044 students**, the largest single hostel intake (`official` — BHEPA USM 14 Sep 2026).
- **Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan (CGH)** operated as an officially unified administrative cluster for 2026 first-year intake, registering **386 students** on Day 3 (14 September 2026) (`official` — BHEPA USM 14 Sep 2026).
- **Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai** operated as a single merged administrative cluster registering **341 students** on Day 1 (12 September 2026) (`official` — HEPA Buletin 12 Sep 2026).
- **5,804 is confirmed as all-campus offers (UPU + international + channels), not Kampus Induk-only** (`official` — Naib Canselor Press Conference 14 Sep 2026).
- **Branch campuses registered on 19 September 2026:** Kampus Kejuruteraan registered 854 students (`official` — BHEPA USM 19 Sep 2026), and Kampus Kesihatan registered 621 students (`official` — USM News KK 19 Sep 2026). Combined 3-campus physical intake is 5,018 students.
- **Physical DTSP Hall Turnout:** Official BHEPA coverage of the Majlis Sambutan Siswa on 18 September 2026 confirmed all Kampus Induk new students attended, framed officially as "kira-kira 3,000 lebih pelajar baharu" (`official` — BHEPA USM 8 Sep 2026; HEPA 18 Sep 2026). No mechanical gate/turnstile count was published.

#### 2. Resolution of Restu, Cahaya Gemilang, and Fajar Harapan

- **Desasiswa Restu:**
  - Registered on Monday, 14 September 2026 (8:30 AM – 5:30 PM).
  - Count: **1,044** new undergraduate students (male and female).
  - Source: Official BHEPA press post dated 14 September 2026 ("Hari Terakhir Pendaftaran Pelajar Baharu Sidang Akademik 2026/2027 Berjalan Lancar").

- **Cahaya Gemilang & Fajar Harapan (Cluster CGH):**
  - Officially designated and managed as **Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan (CGH)** during MSL 2026 intake.
  - Registered on Monday, 14 September 2026 (8:30 AM – 5:30 PM).
  - Count: **386** new students.
  - Clarification on hostel status: First-year students were accommodated under this merged management structure. There was no separate "Fajar Harapan" intake bulletin; all students in that sector were counted in the 386 CGH registration figure.

- **Bakti Fajar Permai Cluster:**
  - First-year students were officially received and housed at Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai (registered on 12 September 2026: **341** students).

#### 3. Kampus Induk vs University-Wide Figures

- **Total University-Wide Offers:** **5,804** students across all campuses (`official` — VC Press Conference 14 Sep 2026).
  - Main UPU Perdana offer: 4,904
  - International full-time: 999 offered
  - SULUNG Program: 216
  - OKU: 17
  - Orang Asli: 8
  - Sports channel: 16
  - IIP Electronic Engineering (housed at Kampus Induk for initial 3 years): 200
- **Kampus Induk Actual Registered Check-In:** **3,543** students across 7 desasiswa (`official`).
- **Branch Campuses Registered Check-In:**
  - Kampus Kejuruteraan (Nibong Tebal): **854** students (registered 19 Sep 2026, `official`).
  - Kampus Kesihatan (Kubang Kerian): **621** students (registered 19 Sep 2026, `official`).
- **All-Campus Physical Intake Sum:** $3,543 + 854 + 621 = 5,018$ registered students (representing an ~86.5% intake take-up against the 5,804 offers).

#### 4. Physical Hall Turnout (DTSP — Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra)

- **Official MSL DTSP Event:** *Majlis Sambutan Siswa Program Siswa Lestari Sidang Akademik 2026/2027* was held on **Friday, 18 September 2026 at 10:00 AM** at Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra (`official` — live-streamed by USMOfficial1969).
- **Audience in Hall:** All registered Kampus Induk new undergraduates (**3,543**) plus 154 PPSL student facilitators, residential Fellows, Senate members, Deans, and university leadership (`official`).
- **Turnout Metric:** Described officially in advance administrative releases as *"kira-kira 3,000 lebih pelajar baharu"* (`official` — BHEPA 8 Sep 2026 release). An exact clicker/RFID door badge count at DTSP entrance doors on 18 Sep 2026 was **not published** publicly.

### 2. HYPOTHESES
- **Restu occupancy density:** Abraham reports Restu rooms are 2 persons per room (`abraham`). With 1,044 first years checking into Restu, this accounts for ~522 occupied rooms during MSL 2026, well within Restu's rated bed capacity of 1,687 to 2,066 beds.
- **Physical DTSP morning attendance:** Modeled as approximately 3,500 students (`assumed`), representing the 3,543 registered first years minus minor absentees (medical bay/quarantine).

### 3. GAPS
- **Door-by-door clicker turnout at DTSP on 18 Sep 2026:** BHEPA published speeches, pledges, and stream archives, but no official digital turnstile tally.
  - *How a human could collect:* Obtain the physical registration log or barcode check-in log maintained by SUKASS / PPSL at the DTSP foyer doors on 18 September morning.
- **Sub-breakdown of Cahaya Gemilang Harapan (CGH):** The 386 students are reported as a single cluster figure. Physical block distribution between Cahaya Gemilang blocks and Fajar Harapan blocks is not segregated in BHEPA's public announcement.
  - *How a human could collect:* Request the block allocation list from Pejabat Penggawa Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan.
