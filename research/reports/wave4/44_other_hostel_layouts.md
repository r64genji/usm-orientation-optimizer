# Other Desasiswa Layouts & Room Inventory (Kampus Induk)

## Topic
Physical layouts, building codes, storeys, vertical transit (lifts vs stairs), gender configurations, room configurations, and accessibility features for the seven non-Restu hostels at USM Kampus Induk: Saujana, Tekun, Indah Kembara, Aman Damai, Bakti Fajar Permai, Cahaya Gemilang, and Fajar Harapan.

**TL;DR:** The non-Restu hostels bifurcate into two distinct architectural typologies: high-rise 10-storey elevator towers at Kompleks RST (Saujana M03–M04 and Tekun M05–M06) and low-rise 2- to 4-storey walk-up staircase blocks inside campus (Indah Kembara L-blocks, Aman Damai K-blocks, Bakti Permai H-blocks, and Cahaya Gemilang Harapan H/F-blocks). In current 2026 administrative housing and check-in rosters, Cahaya Gemilang and Fajar Harapan operate as a single merged administrative cluster (**Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan / CGH**), while Bakti Permai operates under **Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai (BFPP)**.

---

### Key Findings
- **High-rise RST twin towers:** Saujana (M03, M04) and Tekun (M05, M06) are 10-storey high-rise blocks equipped with passenger and fire lifts, opened in 2004 as part of the Kompleks RST development on Halaman Bukit Gambir. ([official], [student-report])
- **Saujana all-female configuration:** Desasiswa Saujana consists exclusively of M03 and M04 and is strictly an all-female desasiswa (~10 storeys each), offering standard twin rooms as well as premium/deluxe room tiers. ([official], [student-report])
- **Tekun gender split:** Desasiswa Tekun comprises M05 (male residents + premium rooms) and M06 (female residents), with central administrative office and food court at M09. ([official], [student-report])
- **Aman Damai walk-up geometry & OKU hub:** Aman Damai comprises 8 low-rise walk-up blocks (K01–K08; 2–3 storeys, no passenger lifts; male K01–K02, female K03–K08) and serves as USM's primary accessible hub, featuring specialized ground-floor (Aras 0) units, wheelchair ramps, accessible restrooms, and covered walkways for OKU/PKU students. ([official], [student-report], [map])
- **Indah Kembara walk-up blocks:** Indah Kembara operates low-rise walk-up residential blocks (L06, L07, L11 for females; L12 for males; administration and meeting rooms at L05; cafeteria/common at L10) with stairs only (no passenger lifts). ([official], [student-report], [map])
- **Merger status of CGH & BFPP:** 
  1. *Cahaya Gemilang Harapan (CGH)* is officially a merged entity combining Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang (H34, H35, office H33) and Desasiswa Harapan (F25, F26, F27), operating under a single Penggawa and MPD CGH, predominantly housing female students with newly renovated premium twin units in F26. ([official], [student-report])
  2. *Bakti Fajar Permai (BFPP)* is a consolidated desasiswa covering Bakti and Permai blocks (H06, H07, H08, H09, office/cafeteria H10, H11, H12, H13, H16, H17, H51) alongside PETAS postgraduate apartments and family housing (K18, K19). ([official], [student-report])

### Details

#### 1. Desasiswa Saujana
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **M03:** Residential tower (OSM way `1031136418`, footprint 3,144.1 m²).
  - **M04:** Residential tower (OSM way `275415000`, footprint 2,173.0 m²; sits higher up the slope).
  - **M07:** Shared central cafeteria / commercial complex (OSM way `275415004`, footprint 878.8 m²).
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - 10 storeys per residential tower (`student-report`, `official`).
  - Equipped with passenger lifts and emergency/fire stairwells.
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Exclusively female (100% perempuan) across both M03 and M04.
- **Room Types:**
  - Standard Twin Rooms (2 single beds, 2 study tables, 2 wardrobes).
  - Premium / Deluxe Rooms (equipped with additional amenities / single occupancy options at higher rate).
- **Published Rates:** Standard Twin RM5.00/day (RM675/sem); Standard Single RM6.00/day (RM810/sem); Luxury/Premium Single RM18.00/day (RM540/month); Luxury Twin RM10.00/day (RM300/month).

#### 2. Desasiswa Tekun
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **M05:** Male residential tower (OSM way `275414999`, footprint 2,481.3 m²; includes Tekun Premium rooms).
  - **M06:** Female residential tower (OSM way `275414998`, footprint 2,206.5 m²).
  - **M09:** Tekun Management Office (Pejabat Penggawa), Dataran Tekun, and cafeteria (OSM way `275415001`, footprint 1,233.6 m²).
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - 10 storeys (`student-report`, `official`).
  - Equipped with passenger lifts and dedicated fire fighting lift (`official`).
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Block M05: Male students (lelaki).
  - Block M06: Female students (perempuan).
- **Room Types:**
  - Standard Twin Rooms (2 occupants per room).
  - Standard Single Rooms.
  - Premium Rooms (Tekun M05 Premium for male undergraduates/postgraduates).
- **Published Rates:** Standard Twin RM5.00/day; Standard Single RM6.00/day; Premium Single RM17.00/day (RM510/month); Premium Twin RM10.00/day (RM300/month).

#### 3. Desasiswa Indah Kembara (IK)
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **L05:** Management office, meeting rooms, Dapur Siswa, facilities (OSM way `275404590`, footprint 1,062.8 m²).
  - **L06:** Female residential block (OSM way `275404589`, footprint 1,643.2 m²).
  - **L07:** Female residential block (OSM way `275404588`, footprint 1,500.8 m²).
  - **L10:** Cafeteria & amenities block.
  - **L11:** Female residential block.
  - **L12:** Male residential block.
  - **K11:** Official residence (Rumah Penggawa IK).
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - Low-rise walk-up blocks: typically 3–4 storeys (`map`, `student-report`).
  - **No passenger lifts**; circulation via external/internal stairwells.
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Female: Blocks L06, L07, L11.
  - Male: Block L12.
- **Room Types:**
  - Predominantly Twin Rooms (2 occupants per room); limited single rooms. Standard rental RM5.00/day (twin) / RM6.00/day (single).

#### 4. Desasiswa Aman Damai
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **K01, K02:** Male residential blocks (OSM way `1030965775`, `275404573`; footprint ~1,037 m² each).
  - **K03, K04, K05, K06, K07, K08:** Female residential blocks (OSM way `building:levels=3`).
  - **K09:** Cafeteria Aman Damai (Cafe Aman / Uncle Hooi).
  - **K10:** Central Administration Office (Pejabat Pengurusan Desasiswa Aman Damai, OSM way `1030965779`, footprint 715 m², 2 storeys).
  - **K12:** Rumah Penggawa Aman Damai.
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - Low-rise walk-up: 2 to 3 storeys (`map` building:levels=3, `student-report`).
  - **No passenger lifts**; access via open stairwells.
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Male: Blocks K01, K02.
  - Female: Blocks K03, K04, K05, K06, K07, K08.
- **OKU / PKU Facilities & Ground Floor Units:**
  - Aman Damai is the primary campus desasiswa retrofitted for Students with Disabilities (Pelajar Kelainan Upaya / OKU).
  - **Ground Floor (Aras 0):** Dedicated OKU ground-floor residential units in Block K01 (male OKU) and K04 (female OKU), eliminating the need for stairs.
  - Equipped with wheelchair ramps connecting blocks to dining halls and roads, tactile paving, grab-railings, and roll-in accessible bathrooms/toilets (`official`).
  - Covered walkway with integrated lighting connects Aman Damai OKU quarters towards academic pathways (`official`).
- **Room Types:** Standard twin rooms (2 single beds, desks, wardrobes) and single rooms.

#### 5. Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai (BFPP)
- **Status / Merger:** Operates as a unified administrative entity (**Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai & PETAS**) managing the historic Bakti and Permai residential blocks along with postgraduate family/executive housing.
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **H06, H07, H08, H09, H11, H12, H13, H16, H17, H51:** Residential blocks (OSM ways `H06`, `H07` show `building:levels=4`; H13 studied in literature as multi-storey masonry).
  - **H10:** Administration Office (Pejabat BFPP) & Cafeteria Bakti Permai (OSM way `275403376`, 2 storeys, footprint 1,264.9 m²).
  - **K18, K19:** Family units / Rumah Keluarga.
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - Multi-storey walk-up blocks (2 to 4 storeys; OSM tags `building:levels=4` on H06/H07; `building:levels=2` on H10; H13 documented as multi-storey). No central passenger lifts in undergraduate blocks (`map`, `official`).
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Male: Blocks H07, H09, H11, H16, H17.
  - Female: Blocks H06, H09 (shared/split), H12, H13, H51.
  - Family / Executive: K18, K19.
- **Room Types:** Standard twin rooms, single rooms, in-suite postgraduate apartments, and family apartments.

#### 6. Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang & Fajar Harapan (Cahaya Gemilang Harapan / CGH)
- **Status / Merger:** 
  - Originally two separate historical desasiswa: Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang (founded 1984) and Desasiswa Harapan (founded 1986).
  - Officially merged by USM into **Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan (CGH)** under a single Penggawa and administration office at Block H33. In 2026 check-in lists and sports contingents, they operate jointly as CGH (`official`).
- **Building Codes & Names:**
  - **Cahaya Gemilang Sector:**
    - **H33:** Administration Office (Pejabat Am CGH, OSM way `275403540`, footprint 813.1 m², 2 storeys).
    - **H34, H35:** Residential blocks (OSM tags show 2 storeys; houses female undergraduates and premium units).
  - **Harapan Sector:**
    - **F25, F26, F27:** Residential and service blocks located adjacent to Tasik Harapan (OSM way `275403371` for F27; F26 underwent major renovation into USM's modern showcase hostel).
- **Storeys & Vertical Transit:**
  - Low-rise walk-up blocks: 2 to 3 storeys (`map`, `student-report`).
  - **No lifts**; stair-based access.
- **Gender Mix:**
  - Exclusively / predominantly female hostel complex (F25, F26, H34, H35).
- **Room Types:**
  - Standard Twin & Single rooms (RM5.00/day twin, RM6.00/day single).
  - Renovated Premium Twin rooms in F26 and H34/H35 (RM280/person/month or RM560/room/month; includes modern furnishings, ceiling fans, high-grade study suites).

### CONFIRMED FACTS
- Desasiswa Saujana consists of two 10-storey blocks, M03 and M04, exclusively housing female students (`official` / `student-report`, 2024–2026, `https://desasiswasaujana.wordpress.com/about`, `https://uess.myusminfo.com/campus-guide-2023/usm-2023/usm-main-campus-2023/usm-2023-accommodation/school-hostel`).
- Desasiswa Tekun consists of two 10-storey blocks, M05 (male) and M06 (female), with administrative offices at M09 (`official`, 2024, `https://tekun.usm.my`, `https://directory.usm.my?direktoristaf%2Fdirektori=&kod=00751`).
- Tekun and Saujana are high-rises served by lifts; Tekun has certified fire fighting lifts (`official`, 2014, `https://www.matec-conferences.org/articles/matecconf/pdf/2014/01/matecconf_bust2013_06002.pdf`).
- Desasiswa Indah Kembara has 4 residential walk-up blocks: L06 (female), L07 (female), L11 (female), and L12 (male); L05 is administrative and L10 is cafeteria (`official`, 2025, `https://hac.usm.my/index.php/indahkembara`, `https://www.facebook.com/DesasiswaIndahKembaraOFFICIAL/posts/1918154571757324`).
- Desasiswa Aman Damai consists of low-rise walk-up blocks K01–K08 (levels=3 on OSM) with K01–K02 for males, K03–K08 for females, and K10 as administrative office (`official` / `map`, 2026, OSM ways `1030965775`, `275404573`, `1030965779`).
- Desasiswa Aman Damai houses the primary campus OKU/PKU accessible units on Aras 0 (ground floor) with wheelchair ramps, accessible toilets, and covered walkways (`official`, 2025, `https://hepa.usm.my/index.php/kebajikan/buddy-pelajar-oku-tingkatkan-semangat-perpaduan-di-kalangan-pelajar-terapkan-capaian-pendidikan-berkualiti-sdg-4`, `https://malaysiagazette.com/2025/09/29/pelajar-oku-autism-teruja-mula-pengajian-di-usm`).
- Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang and Desasiswa Harapan operate administratively as a merged desasiswa (**Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang Harapan / CGH**) with office at H33 and residential blocks F25, F26, H34, H35 (`official`, 2026, `https://directory.usm.my?direktoristaf%2Fdirektori=&kod=01433`, `https://hepa.usm.my/index.php/servis/hac`).
- Standard room configuration across all Kampus Induk undergraduate hostels is twin occupancy (2 students per room) priced at RM5.00/day (RM675/semester) (`official` / `student-report`, 2024, `https://uess.myusminfo.com/campus-guide-2023/usm-2023/usm-main-campus-2023/usm-2023-accommodation/school-hostel`).
