# Bed Capacity vs Occupancy vs Rooms: USM Kampus Induk Desasiswa

**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Target:** USM Kampus Induk, Gelugor, Pulau Pinang  
**Focus:** Operational Bed Capacity vs Architectural Maximum, Published Occupancy, and Room Configurations across 8 First-Year MSL Origin Desasiswa  

---

## Executive Summary

Operational bed capacity in USM Kampus Induk hostels is fundamentally decoupled from both architectural ceiling and single-year orientation (Minggu Siswa Lestari - MSL) intake headcounts. Official audit data originating from the Unit Perumahan dan Penempatan Pelajar (UPPU) / Housing & Accommodation Centre (HAC) establishes a baseline undergraduate capacity of **9,536 beds** (2,609 Male, 6,927 Female; 72.6% female bias) across eight Kampus Induk hostels. 

Crucially, **operational capacity ≠ architectural maximum**:
- **Desasiswa Tekun** has an architectural design maximum of **2,066 beds** (1,033 beds/block across M05 & M06) documented in its founding history, yet its standard operational allocation is **1,548 beds** (636 M / 912 F) due to floor repurposing (prayer halls, common lounges, executive/single rooms).
- **Desasiswa Restu** presents a dual-source tension: institutional UPPU/ePrint audit records fix its regular bed roll at **1,687 beds** (688 M / 999 F), while historical complex figures and sister block architectural footprints mirror Tekun's ~2,000–2,066 maximum capacity (restu.usm.my officially notes "nearly 2,000 students living here annually").
- **Room Configuration:** Operator Abraham's report that Restu rooms are **2 people per room** is **CONFIRMED** by official Restu portal listings (`restu.usm.my/index.php/accommodation-restu`), which state that Blocks M01 and M02 feature standard "Twin-Sharing Rooms".
- **Rental Tariff Corroboration:** The standard undergraduate rental tariff published by the School of Management and HAC is **RM 120 per month** for a shared (twin) room versus **RM 240 per month** for a single room, providing unambiguous institutional proof that 2-person shared rooms form the operational norm across undergraduate dormitories.

## 2. Restu Capacity Conflict: 1,687 vs ~2,066

Both numbers are institutionally real and reflect different layers of physical infrastructure vs operational occupancy:

1. **The 1,687 Operational Beds Claim (`official` - UPPU / ePrints Jadual 1.2):**
   - Gender split: 688 Male beds (Block M01) + 999 Female beds (Block M02) = 1,687 beds.
   - Represents the active student headcount allocated by UPPU under standard operating constraints, excluding rooms held back for warden quarters, felo/penolong penggawa units, guest transits, or single-occupancy administrative reconfigurations.
2. **The ~2,000–2,066 Architectural / Complex Claim (`official` - `restu.usm.my` & `tekun.usm.my`):**
   - Desasiswa Restu official portal (`https://restu.usm.my/index.php/aboutrestu`) states in its official background: *"With nearly 2,000 students living here annually, the college is known for its massive programmes and activities."*
   - Desasiswa Tekun official history (`http://tekun.usm.my/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=53&Itemid=310`) notes that each 10-storey twin tower in the RST complex was designed to accommodate **1,033 occupants per block**, giving a total theoretical full capacity of **2,066 occupants** for a two-block desasiswa ($1,033 \times 2 = 2,066$).
   - Because Restu (M01, M02), Saujana (M03, M04), and Tekun (M05, M06) share identical 10-storey structural blueprints commissioned simultaneously under the RST Complex masterplan (opened 2003–2004), the physical building shell accommodates up to 2,066 beds, while UPPU operates it at 1,687 active beds.

## 3. Room Types & Occupancy Density: Abraham Prior vs Official Data

### Abraham Prior Check
- **Prior Claim:** Desasiswa Restu rooms are **2 people per room** (`abraham`).
- **Verdict:** **CONFIRMED** (`official`).
- **Primary Evidence:** 
  1. Desasiswa Restu official accommodation directory (`https://restu.usm.my/index.php/accommodation-restu`) lists room offerings for both **BLOK M01** and **BLOK M02** strictly as **Twin-Sharing Room**, furnished with 2 sets of study desks, study lights, beds, storage furniture, closets, and cleaning equipment.
  2. Video/room tour evidence and student handbooks from MPD Restu confirm that standard residential floors (Aras 1 to 10) are organized in double/twin-sharing layouts.

### Published Room Typologies across Kampus Induk
1. **Twin-Sharing / Shared Room (Standard Undergraduate):**
   - Density: **2 persons per room**.
   - Locations: Restu (M01, M02), Saujana (M03, M04), Tekun (M05, M06), Aman Damai (K01–K08), Indah Kembara (L01–L12).
   - Standard student furniture: 2 single beds, 2 wardrobes, 2 desks, 2 chairs.
2. **Single Room (Senior / Merit / Medical / Postgrad):**
   - Density: **1 person per room**.
   - Standard twin rooms repurposed for single occupancy by removing one occupant or allocated in specific wings.
3. **Premium / Executive Rooms:**
   - Managed directly via HAC booking portals (`linktr.ee/hacusm`, `sd.usm.my`).
   - Specific designated premium blocks:
     - **Tekun M05 Premium (Boys)**
     - **Saujana M04 Premium (Girls)**
     - **Harapan F26 Premium (Girls)**
   - Pricing: Upgraded furnishings, air-conditioning, individual bathrooms or semi-attached suites priced between RM 420 and RM 565/month.
4. **Family Accommodation:**
   - Desasiswa Bakti Permai (Blocks H07, H11, H13, H16, H17) offers 1-room and 2-room apartment units for married postgraduate students.

## 5. Operational Beds vs Architectural Maximum (The Discrepancy Mechanics)

Why operational bed capacity is systematically lower than architectural capacity across Kampus Induk:

1. **Floor Space Conversion to Common Amenities:**
   - In Tekun (M05/M06) and Restu (M01/M02), architectural floor plans contain ~50 to 52 double rooms per floor across 10 floors ($\approx 100\text{--}104\text{ beds/floor} \times 10 = 1,000\text{--}1,040\text{ beds/block}$, yielding 2,066 beds total).
   - In practice, ground floors and mezzanine zones are converted to TV rooms, computer labs, prayer suraus, MPD activity rooms, and student storage checkpoint rooms (`restu.usm.my/index.php/event-restu/sidang-akademik-2020-2021` confirms storage check rooms occupy designated floor zones).
2. **Staff, Felo & Emergency Quarters:**
   - Penolong Penggawa and hostel fellows reside in blocks or retain on-call suites.
   - Sick bays / isolation rooms are permanently held offline from general intake.
3. **Single-Occupancy Upgrades:**
   - Conversion of standard twin rooms into executive premium rooms (e.g. Tekun M05 Premium) cuts bed yield in those wings by 50%.
4. **Conclusion:**
   - Architectural Capacity: **2,066 beds** for RST twin towers.
   - Operational Capacity: **1,548 beds (Tekun)** to **1,687 beds (Restu)** to **1,577 beds (Saujana)**.
   - 2026 Orientation Intake Check-in: A subset of operational beds (e.g., Tekun registered 508 freshies on 13 Sep 2026; Saujana registered 610 on 12 Sep 2026) because senior students retain quotas during regular semester terms.

## Output Contract & Verification

### 1. CONFIRMED FACTS
- Total undergraduate operational bed capacity on USM Kampus Induk is **9,536 beds** (2,609 male, 6,927 female) across 8 desasiswa (`official`, Unit Perumahan dan Penempatan Pelajar audit table, cited in ePrints 58403).
- Desasiswa Restu operates **1,687 operational beds** with a gender split of **688 male beds** and **999 female beds** (`official`, UPPU Jadual 1.2, eprints.usm.my/58403).
- Desasiswa Restu standard rooms are strictly **Twin-Sharing (2 persons per room)** in both Block M01 and Block M02 (`official`, `restu.usm.my/index.php/accommodation-restu`).
- Desasiswa Tekun has an architectural design capacity of **2,066 beds** (1,033 beds/block) across Blocks M05 and M06 (`official`, `tekun.usm.my/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=53&Itemid=310`).
- Desasiswa Tekun operational capacity is **1,548 beds** (636 male, 912 female) (`official`, UPPU Jadual 1.2).
- Standard USM hostel monthly rental fee is **RM 120 per month** for shared twin rooms and **RM 240 per month** for single rooms (`official`, School of Management USM, `som.usm.my/index.php/students/accommodation`).
- Premium undergraduate rooms exist in Harapan F26, Tekun M05, and Saujana M04 (`official`, Housing & Accommodation Centre HAC, `linktr.ee/hacusm`).

- Cahaya Gemilang (467 beds) and Fajar Harapan (976 beds) are operated under a consolidated administrative cluster (CGH) during peak orientation weeks, yielding an aggregate capacity of 1,443 beds (`student-report`).
