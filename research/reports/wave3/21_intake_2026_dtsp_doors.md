# 2026/2027 USM Intake & DTSP Hall Exterior Portals Report

## Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) — Kampus Induk Intake (2026/2027) & DTSP Door Architecture

**TL;DR:** For Sidang Akademik 2026/2027, USM offered places to **5,804 new undergraduate students** overall (4,904 via UPU Perdana, with 200 placed in Kampus Induk for the new Electronic Engineering IIP programme), though a standalone, official Induk-only grand total remains unsegregated in press releases; Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra (DTSP) architecturally features **4 primary perimeter exit portals (A', B', C', D')** feeding into **6 exterior egress corridors (Corridors 1, 2, 4, 5, 14, 15)** alongside internal lateral links (3b, 3c, 12, 13).

---

### Key Findings

- **USM Undergraduate Intake 2026/2027:** On 14 September 2026, USM officially announced that **5,804** STPM/Matrikulasi/Asasi/Diploma school-leavers were offered places for full-time undergraduate and diploma programmes across all campuses, including 4,904 via UPU Saluran Perdana ([BHEPA USM](https://hepa.usm.my/index.php/berita/buletin/usm-perkasa-akses-pendidikan-5-804-pelajar-baharu-ditawarkan-tempat-bagi-sidang-akademik-2026-2027) / [USM News Portal](https://news.usm.my/berita-mutakhir/5-804-pelajar-baharu-mulakan-langkah-di-usm-pendidikan-inklusif-jadi-keutamaan)).
- **Kampus Induk Segregated Count Status (NOT FOUND / PARTIALLY DISCLOSED):** An exact standalone total for *Kampus Induk only* across all faculties was not officially published as a discrete single number in the 2026 intake announcement; however, specific hostel registration counts on Kampus Induk were published for 12–13 September 2026: **Saujana (610)**, **Indah Kembara (244)**, **Bakti Fajar Permai (341)**, **Aman Damai (410)**, and **Tekun (508)**, totaling **2,113** registered new students across these 5 complexes alone ([BHEPA Bulletin 12 Sep 2026](https://hepa.usm.my/index.php/berita/buletin/usm-sambut-kedatangan-1196-pelajar-baharu-sidang-akademik-2026-2027); [BHEPA Bulletin 13 Sep 2026](https://hepa.usm.my/index.php/berita/buletin/proses-pendaftaran-pelajar-baharu-usm-dapat-maklumbalas-positif-dalam-kalangan-ibu-bapa)).
- **Prior Year Intake Benchmarks (Properly Labeled):**
  - **2025/2026:** 4,236 new undergraduate students university-wide ([USM Official Announcement, 22 Sep 2025](https://www.facebook.com/USMOfficial1969/posts/strong-local-enrolment-rising-global-interest-usm-reaffirms-commitment-to-malays/1296500115850068)).
  - **2024/2025:** 4,046 new undergraduate students university-wide ([USM Official Announcement, 22 Sep 2025](https://www.facebook.com/USMOfficial1969/posts/strong-local-enrolment-rising-global-interest-usm-reaffirms-commitment-to-malays/1296500115850068)).
  - **2023/2024:** 4,025 new undergraduate students university-wide ([USM Official Announcement, 22 Sep 2025](https://www.facebook.com/USMOfficial1969/posts/strong-local-enrolment-rising-global-interest-usm-reaffirms-commitment-to-malays/1296500115850068)).
- **DTSP Physical Door Architecture:** Academic crowd egress modeling and topological layout analysis of DTSP (Khalid et al., Springer *Discrete Event Dyn Syst*, DOI:10.1007/s10626-015-0215-0) establishes that the auditorium is served by **4 exterior exit portals / perimeter gates (labeled A', B', C', and D')** connecting directly to the outside and foyer circulation:
  - Exits A' and B' feed exterior corridors 1 and 2 (left flank).
  - Exits C' and D' feed exterior corridors 4 and 5 (right flank).
  - Corridors 14 and 15 provide high-capacity direct egress portals from the rear/foyer concourse (Pintu Utama / Foyer Dataran Merah side).
- **First-Year Desasiswa Hostels (Kampus Induk):** First-year undergraduate students on the Main Campus (Gelugor) are housed across the following primary residential colleges (Desasiswa):
  - **RST Cluster:** Desasiswa Restu, Desasiswa Saujana, Desasiswa Tekun (located across the highway/bypass, requiring bus transit to DTSP).
  - **Main Campus Core Hostels:** Desasiswa Aman Damai (OKU-equipped and sports admissions), Desasiswa Bakti Permai (Bakti Fajar), Desasiswa Indah Kembara, Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang.

### Details

#### 1. Official 2026/2027 Undergraduate Intake Breakdown

During the press conference chaired by Vice-Chancellor Prof. Dato' Seri Ir. Dr. Abdul Rahman Mohamed at Dewan Utama Desasiswa (DUD) M08 RST on 14 September 2026:
- **Total Intake Offered (All Campuses):** 5,804 students from STPM, Matrikulasi, Asasi, and Diploma qualifications.
- **UPU Saluran Perdana:** 4,904 candidates accepted out of 37,171 applicants across 79 Bachelor's degree programmes.
- **Special Enrolment Streams:**
  - B40 Category: 2,241 candidates across the three USM campuses (46% of total UPU offers).
  - Program Siswa SULUNG: 216 first-in-family students (allocation: RM2.16 million).
  - OKU (Persons with Disabilities): 17 candidates (4 male, 13 female), accommodated with dedicated facilities at Desasiswa Aman Damai.
  - Orang Asli: 8 candidates.
  - Athletes: 16 state/national athletes.
  - Industry-Infused Programme (IIP) Bachelor of Electronic Engineering: Cohort of 200 students temporarily based at **Kampus Induk** before migrating to SAINS@USM (Bukit Jambul) in 2029.
  - International Undergraduate Offers: 999 international candidates admitted across 37 countries.

#### 2. Architectural Floor Plan & Exterior Portals of Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra (DTSP)

Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra is the grand auditorium of USM Kampus Induk, designed with an octagonal/faceted auditorium floor and tiered balcony seating. According to architectural modeling of crowd evacuation topologies (Khalid et al., *Discrete Event Dynamic Systems* 2016, 26:603–631):
- **Core Topological Layout:**
  - DTSP contains **15 network circulation branches** inside the main hall: 6 source corridors (internal seating aisles 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11), 3 intermediate connectors (Corridor 3a, 12, 13), and **6 primary exterior exit conduits** (Corridors 1, 2, 4, 5, 14, 15).
  - **4 Major Exterior Portals / Perimeter Exits:**
    - **Exit A' & B':** Located on the northern / lateral flank (flanking seating zones W & X), opening through Corridors 1 & 2 toward exterior covered walkways and side apron.
    - **Exit C' & D':** Located on the southern / lateral flank (flanking seating zones Y & Z), opening through Corridors 4 & 5 toward the side circulation plaza.
  - **Main Foyer / Concourse Doors (Corridors 14 & 15):** The rear/main foyer elevation facing Dataran Merah (the grand formal entrance / Pintu Utama) contains high-capacity egress portal sets (Corridors 14 and 15) that exhibit zero blocking under standard flow due to wide double-leaf glass doors opening out into the spacious DTSP foyer and Dataran Merah concourse.
  - In convocation and formal university ceremonies, protocol guidelines mandate guest and student entry designated by specific portal letters (Pintu A, Pintu B, Pintu C, and Pintu D / Foyer) to distribute ingress across the four quadrants of the hall.

#### 3. First-Year Hostels (Desasiswa) on Kampus Induk

First-year students attending Minggu Siswa Lestari (MSL) are allocated rooms across the following hostels:
1. **Desasiswa Saujana (RST Cluster):** 610 registered on 12 Sep 2026; single/shared blocks across the expressway.
2. **Desasiswa Tekun (RST Cluster):** 508 registered on 13 Sep 2026; multi-storey high-density block.
3. **Desasiswa Restu (RST Cluster):** Core bus transit loading origin for the western campus perimeter.
4. **Desasiswa Aman Damai:** 410 registered on 13 Sep 2026; houses OKU students, sports athletes, and general arts/sciences freshmen.
5. **Desasiswa Indah Kembara:** 244 registered on 12 Sep 2026; situated on the main campus ring.
6. **Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai:** 341 registered on 12 Sep 2026; core residential compound.
7. **Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang:** Traditional campus residential block.
