# Main-Campus Desasiswa Routes to Dewan Tuanku Syed Putra (DTSP)

**TL;DR:** All eight main-campus USM hostels send first-year cohorts to DTSP via two distinct transit regimes: western high-ridge hostels (Restu, Saujana, Tekun; 1.8–2.6 km away) rely on single-door diesel charter coaches along Jalan Universiti and the Padang Kawad covered skybridge, while central and eastern hostels (Indah Kembara, Aman Damai, Bakti Permai, Cahaya Gemilang, Fajar Harapan; 0.68–1.41 km away) rely predominantly on escorted walking corridors. Bottlenecks across all corridors converge at narrow multi-flight concrete stairs, unshaded 1.2-meter arterial sidewalks, single bus loading bays, and the ultimate unshaded exterior roadway reservoir outside DTSP.

---

### Key Findings
- **Transit Regime Split:** Western ridge hostels (RST: Restu, Saujana, Tekun; ~1,700–3,000 residents) are the primary source of bus shuttle demand due to 1.8–2.6 km distance and a steep ridge ascent/descent, whereas hostels within a 1.5 km radius (Aman Damai, Bakti Permai, Cahaya Gemilang, Fajar Harapan, Indah Kembara) move primarily via guided pedestrian columns led by Pembimbing Program Siswa Lestari (PPSL) ([MPP USM Archive 1020040901450894](https://www.facebook.com/mppusm.official/photos/laluan-bas-e-laluan-ungujejantas-tekun-m07-saujana-m01-restu-jejantas/1020040901450894); [USM Official Archive 1114284630738285](https://www.facebook.com/USMOfficial1969/posts/1114284630738285)).
- **Primary Pedestrian Spine:** Jalan Universiti serves as the east-west campus arterial trunk connecting all southern and western hostels to the DTSP concourse via Jalan Perpustakaan and the PHS 1 plaza ([OSM Way 646871890](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/646871890)).
- **Skybridge Chokepoints:** The RST ridge is connected to the central campus basin at Padang Kawad via the covered pedestrian footbridge *Jejantas Padang Kawad / Tekun* ([OSM Way 1030376052](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1030376052)). The bridge deck spans two lanes of perimeter road, but egress at both ends narrows into steep concrete stairways ([OSM Ways 1030376053, 1030376054, 1030376056](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1030376053)), throttling large pedestrian platoons to single-file flow.
- **Vehicles Observed:** Charter high-floor diesel tour coaches (UNIC Leisure Transtour, 40–44 seats, ~20 standing crush capacity, 60–65 total) with **exactly 1 front entrance door** and a 3-step internal stairwell. No low-floor electric shuttles or multi-door articulated buses are deployed on orientation bulk routes ([Report 23](23_vehicle_types_capacity.md); [Boarding Video 07:49](raw_data/media/videos/2026-09-17_0749_bus_boarding_queue.mp4)).
- **Physical Road Bottlenecks:** Single-lane pick-up bays at hostel forecourts (e.g. M08 Restu forecourt, K10 Aman Damai stop), lack of continuous tree canopy or sheltered awnings along Jalan Universiti, and an absence of physical queue railings outside DTSP force hundreds of students to spill onto active vehicular asphalt under intense morning sun ([DTSP Video 08:06](raw_data/media/videos/2026-09-17_0806_dtsp_exterior_reservoir.mp4)).

### Details

#### 1. Western Ridge Hostel Cluster (RST: Restu, Saujana, Tekun)

##### Desasiswa Restu (M01)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3558402, 100.2891963 ([OSM Way 139062076](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/139062076))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 2,282 m street route (OSRM walk: ~30.4–34.2 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h; crow-fly: 1.47 km).
- **Physical Path:** Exits the western accommodation towers onto Halaman Bukit Gambir 4, descending along the perimeter ring road eastward toward Padang Kawad. For walking, students cross the elevated covered footbridge (*Jejantas Padang Kawad*, OSM way 1030376052) descending directly into Padang Kawad hub, then follow the northern sidewalk of Jalan Universiti eastward past Jabatan Keselamatan and Pusat Bio, turning north into Jalan Perpustakaan to reach the DTSP concourse.
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Restu Departure Pavilion / M08 Forecourt (`5.3572768, 100.2898076`) and lower road shoulder boarding berth near Padang Kawad perimeter (`5.356013, 100.293685`).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - *Single-file boarding choke:* One single-door coach bay at the road shoulder. Boarding video `2026-09-17_0749_bus_boarding_queue.mp4` shows single-file boarding through a 75 cm doorway requiring 105 seconds per bus (1.9–2.1 s/pax).
  - *Road shoulder queuing:* Lack of pedestrian barrier/guardrail forces the overflow queue to line up directly on the vehicular asphalt shoulder (`2026-09-17_0737_road_shoulder_queue.mp4`).
  - *Elevation & heat exposure:* Walking requires ascending/descending a steep 30-meter elevation grade on the western ridge with unshaded gaps between canopy clusters.

##### Desasiswa Saujana (M07)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3564, 100.2905 ([USM Campus Map](https://maps.usm.my/main))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 1,782 m street route (OSRM walk: ~23.8–26.7 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Located between Restu and Tekun on the western ridge. Pedestrian path leads eastward down Saujana access lanes straight to the western portal of *Jejantas Padang Kawad*, descending stair towers into the Padang Kawad transit clearing, then moving along Jalan Universiti sidewalk to Jalan Perpustakaan and DTSP.
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Shared RST forecourt loop and M07 Saujana pavilion ([MPP USM Laluan E Archive](https://www.facebook.com/mppusm.official/photos/laluan-bas-e-laluan-ungujejantas-tekun-m07-saujana-m01-restu-jejantas/1020040901450894)).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Merges into the same single-lane bus loop as Restu; buses arriving at Saujana are often already at seated capacity from Restu, forcing standee boarding or queue rejection.
  - Staircase descending from Jejantas to Padang Kawad (`OSM way 1030376054`) is 1.5 m wide with concrete handrails, forming a funnel when large student platoons disembark.

##### Desasiswa Tekun (M05)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3556294, 100.2912928 ([OSM Way 275414999](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/275414999))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 2,599 m street route (OSRM walk: ~34.7–39.0 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Exits Tekun tower base north-east toward Saujana loop or directly through the covered pedestrian link to Jejantas Tekun. Traverses the elevated bridge across the perimeter bypass road into Padang Kawad hub, proceeding along Jalan Universiti to DTSP.
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Tekun ridge forecourt / M05 boarding berth ([TikTok @mpdtekun](https://www.tiktok.com/@mpdtekun/video/7598181484171857160)).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Longest walking path in the residential portfolio (2.6 km); walking in MSL formal attire (long pants/baju kurung, leather shoes) induces severe thermal discomfort.
  - Bus queueing at Tekun experiences high dwell variance due to tight turnaround geometry for 12-meter tour coaches at the ridge cul-de-sac.

#### 2. Central & Southern Hostels (Padang Kawad / Eureka Precinct)

##### Desasiswa Indah Kembara (L05, L06, L07)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.356033, 100.2960407 ([OSM Way 275404588](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/275404588))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 1,414 m street route (OSRM walk: ~18.9–21.2 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Located on flat ground directly bordering Padang Kawad. Walking path exits east along Jalan Indah Kembara, immediately connecting to Jalan Universiti. Follows Jalan Universiti past INFORMM and the Chemistry faculty, turning north onto Jalan Perpustakaan directly into the DTSP forecourt ([TikTok @desasiswaindahkembara](https://www.tiktok.com/@desasiswaindahkembara/video/7547337528458710279)).
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Hub Padang Kawad / Bus Stop Desasiswa Indah Kembara ([OSM Node 1129299988 / 11880869101](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1129299988), ref 12003018).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Flat terrain makes walking viable, but the unshaded stretch of Jalan Universiti between Jabatan Keselamatan and INFORMM lacks canopy cover.
  - The pedestrian crossing at the intersection of Jalan Indah Kembara and Jalan Universiti is unsignalized; student columns face conflicts with orientation shuttle buses and staff vehicles.

##### Desasiswa Aman Damai (K01–K10)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3543055, 100.2961546 ([OSM Way 1030965779](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1030965779))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 1,136 m street route (OSRM walk: ~15.1–17.0 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Located south of Padang Kawad near the USM stadium / swimming pool complex. Pedestrian route proceeds north from Damai Cafeteria (K10) along Jalan Damai, turns right onto Jalan Universiti near Jabatan Keselamatan ([OSM Node 11880869103](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11880869103)), then follows the main sidewalk east along Jalan Universiti to Jalan Perpustakaan and DTSP.
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Aman-Damai Bus Stop / Komuter Bus Stop K10 ([OSM Node 1129300447 / 11880869102](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1129300447), ref 3 / 12003028).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Social posts ([Instagram @amandamai.usm](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdLpeiek6bt)) show PPSL marshals gathering cohorts in the K10 cafeteria plaza and marching in dense platoons along the narrow 1.2 m sidewalk of Jalan Damai.
  - Footpath width on Jalan Damai is insufficient for two-way columns, spilling students into roadside gutters.
  - The bus stop at K10 has a small sheltered bench with capacity for ~15 persons; orientation platoons (>150 students) wait under open sun if awaiting buses.

##### Desasiswa Fajar Harapan (F26, F27)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3550006, 100.2997724 ([OSM Way 275403371](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/275403371))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 680 m street route (OSRM walk: ~9.1–10.2 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Shortest walking distance among all hostels. Pedestrian path exits F26/F27 residential blocks northeast along Eureka access lanes, skirting the USM central lake (Tasik USM), past Dewan Budaya to enter the southern side entrance of DTSP directly ([Facebook USM Official 1114284630738285](https://www.facebook.com/USMOfficial1969/posts/1114284630738285)).
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Bus Stop Desasiswa Fajar Harapan ([OSM Node 1129298776](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/1129298776), ref 12003026) and Stor Pusat Kimia ([OSM Node 12191248268](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/12191248268)). Buses are rarely used for MSL transit due to the sub-10-minute walk.
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Multiple short outdoor concrete staircases ([OSM Ways 1354561992, 1353883015](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1354561992)) navigate grade steps from Fajar residential terraces to the upper Eureka campus plane.
  - The walkway beside Tasik USM lacks railings along certain edge sectors and becomes congested when cohorts pause to reorganize lines.

#### 3. Northern & North-Eastern Hostels (Science Precinct & Minden Sports Complex)

##### Desasiswa Bakti Permai (H09–H13)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3577556, 100.3005464 ([OSM Way 275403376](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/275403376))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 972 m street route (OSRM walk: ~13.0–14.6 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Exits H10 cafeteria block eastward via Persiaran Sains through the School of Chemical Sciences and Pusat Sejahtera complex ([OSM Node 11880869105](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11880869105)), crossing into Jalan Perpustakaan and entering the DTSP north-western concourse directly opposite PHS 1 ([TikTok @bfp.ontiktok](https://www.tiktok.com/@bfp.ontiktok/video/7657955778611137813)).
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Desasiswa Bakti Fajar Permai Petas Bus Stop ([OSM Node 9498085667](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/9498085667), ref 12003021) along Persiaran Sains.
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Route passes through dense faculty clusters featuring numerous covered walkway stairways ([OSM Ways 1354565634, 1354565641, 1353883021](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1354565634)) with 3 to 12 concrete steps, breaking pedestrian cadence and causing accordion bunching in marching columns.
  - Footpath connects through narrow breezeways between Chemistry laboratories where corridor width narrows to ~1.2 meters.

##### Desasiswa Cahaya Gemilang (H33–H35)
- **Origin Coordinates:** 5.3604563, 100.3035977 ([OSM Way 275403540](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/275403540))
- **Distance to DTSP:** 784 m street route (OSRM walk: ~10.5–11.8 min at 4.0–4.5 km/h).
- **Physical Path:** Located on the northern perimeter near Bukit Gambir gate and Minden Sports Complex (Pusat Sukan). Cohorts exit H33/H34 southward down Jalan Gemilang and Jalan Mahasiswa, pass DK Foyer and Kompleks Dewan Kuliah C23 ([OSM Node 11880869106](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/11880869106)), merge onto Lengkok Sastera to Jalan Perpustakaan, arriving at DTSP north portico ([YouTube @fourartroom](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7fHzYMX1orw)).
- **Bus Staging & Pick-up Point:** Kompleks Dewan Kuliah C23 bus shelter (OSM Node 11880869106).
- **Observed Bottlenecks:**
  - Steep terrain descent from the high northern sports ridge toward the central library depression; stairs near DK complex ([OSM Ways 1354921230, 1354673739](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1354921230)) create pinch points during rapid mass descent.
  - Concourse merges directly with the heavy pedestrian stream from PHS 1 and School of Humanities.

#### 4. The Destination Bottleneck: DTSP Exterior Roadway & Holding Reservoir
- **Drop-Off & Reservoir Coordinates:** 5.3568866, 100.3030831 ([OSM Way 896393762](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/896393762))
- **Geometry & Physical Reality:**
  - The drop-off curb along Jalan Perpustakaan has space for exactly **one to two buses** to alight simultaneously.
  - When buses drop off cohorts from Restu/Tekun, students do not enter DTSP immediately.
  - Video footage in `2026-09-17_0806_dtsp_exterior_reservoir.mp4` and `video_fedd16a3e55d.mp4` shows a massive holding reservoir of **300 to 400+ students held stationary on the open two-lane asphalt roadway outside DTSP**.
  - Ground conditions show zero shade canopy, no portable mist fans, and no temporary awnings.
  - Footpath access into the main entrance doors of DTSP is constricted by two double-leaf glass doors flanked by bag-check / registration counters, processing only ~30 students per minute against an arrival rate exceeding 80 students per minute.

---

#### 5. Cross-Check with Local Media & Vehicle Characteristics
- **Vehicle Types:**
  - All buses observed in `raw_data/media/videos/2026-09-17_0749_bus_boarding_queue.mp4` and student posts are standard 12-meter commercial high-floor tour coaches (*bas persiaran*) in purple/white UNIC Leisure Transtours / USM livery.
  - There are **no low-floor city transit buses** and **no electric shuttles** operating on this mass trunk corridor.
- **Crush Loading & Standees:**
  - Telemetry and video evidence corroborate that every bus operates at crush capacity: ~44 seated passengers plus 15–20 standees tightly packed down the 45 cm center aisle, holding headrest grab handles. Total passenger load: ~60–65 per coach.
- **Boarding Dwell Time:**
  - Timestamps from `2026-09-17_0749_bus_boarding_queue.mp4` and Sensor Logger GPS verify dwell time of **~1 minute 45 seconds (105 seconds)** to board 50–55 students through the single narrow 3-step front entrance door.
